Graduation Project

APMS & Roshetety

An advanced pharmacy ecosystem bridging the gap between digital prescription submission and robotic dispensing.

The Problem

For Patients

  • Paper dependency: Physical prescriptions required.
  • Long wait times: Waiting in line for processing.
  • Zero visibility: No way to know when an order is ready.

For Pharmacies

  • Manual data entry: Slow and prone to human error.
  • Phantom stock: Digital records out of sync with physical stock.
  • Inefficient dispensing: Time wasted locating medicines.

The Solution

A unified ecosystem comprising a patient-facing mobile app and an intelligent pharmacy dashboard.

Roshetety App

Digital prescription submission via OCR or manual entry with real-time WebSocket order tracking.

APMS Dashboard

Staff UI for inventory management, AI-driven demand forecasting, and smart reordering.

Robotic Integration

Physical dispensing hardware controlled via the APMS gateway, reducing manual fetch time.

User Persona: The Patient

Sarah (34)

Busy working mother who manages prescriptions for her children and elderly parents.

Goals & Frustrations

  • Goals: Wants to drop off prescriptions without waiting in line. Needs to know exactly when to pick them up.
  • Frustrations: Arriving at the pharmacy only to find the medicine is out of stock. Wasting time waiting for preparation.

User Persona: The Pharmacist

Ahmed (42)

Lead pharmacist managing inventory, staff, and a high volume of daily prescriptions.

Goals & Frustrations

  • Goals: Efficiently dispense medication, maintain accurate inventory, and optimize reordering.
  • Frustrations: Illegible handwriting on paper prescriptions. Discrepancies between physical and digital stock.

User Journey: Patient

1. Scan Prescription

Sarah uses the Roshetety app to photograph a paper prescription. The AI extracts medicine names.

2. Review & Submit

She verifies the extracted medicines and submits the order digitally to the APMS gateway.

3. Live Tracking

She monitors the order status via WebSockets and goes to the pharmacy only when notified it is ready.

User Journey: Pharmacist

1. Receive Order

Ahmed sees the incoming digital order on the APMS Dashboard with pre-validated medicine names.

2. Link & Dispatch

He links the request to physical inventory and taps 'Dispatch', sending coordinates to the robot.

3. Dispense & Notify

The robot fetches the items. The system deducts stock and automatically notifies Sarah her order is ready.
