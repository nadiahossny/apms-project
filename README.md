# 💊 Roshetety & PharmaSys: Smart Prescription Scanner & Pharmacy Management App

## 🚀 Overview
**Roshetety** is a cutting-edge mobile application designed to bridge the gap between patients and pharmacies. Built as an integral part of the **Advanced Pharmacy Management System (APMS)**, the app leverages Artificial Intelligence and real-time data sync to modernize how medical prescriptions are processed, reducing human error and saving valuable time.

## 🧠 The Challenge
For decades, pharmacies have struggled with manually transcribing handwritten or printed prescriptions. This traditional process is time-consuming, prone to critical reading errors, and creates bottlenecks in customer service. We needed a solution that could instantly digitize prescriptions while maintaining a seamless, user-friendly experience for both patients and pharmacists.

## 💡 The Solution
We designed Roshetety with a mobile-first, scan-and-go philosophy. By integrating advanced OCR (Optical Character Recognition) and real-time communication, the app allows users to simply snap a photo of a prescription or scan a QR code. The UI is intentionally minimalist, ensuring that users of all technical backgrounds can navigate the app without friction.

## ✨ Key Features & UX Highlights
*   **🤖 AI-Powered OCR Scanning:** A sleek, guided camera interface that uses Google ML Kit to instantly extract text from uploaded or photographed prescriptions.
*   **📷 Smart QR & Barcode Reader:** Lightning-fast QR scanning for modern e-prescriptions and medication tracking.
*   **⚡ Real-Time Syncing:** Live updates powered by WebSockets, ensuring that the moment a prescription is scanned, the pharmacy dashboard is instantly updated.
*   **📱 Frictionless Image Picker:** A smooth gallery and camera integration flow for uploading existing medical documents.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy
*   **Clean & Accessible:** A medical app needs to evoke trust and clarity. We focused on a clean layout, generous whitespace, and highly legible typography.
*   **Guided Interactions:** Scanning documents can be tricky for some users, so we designed intuitive overlays and micro-animations to guide the user's camera positioning.
*   **Error Prevention:** Clear validation states and easy manual overrides in case the AI needs a little help reading messy doctor handwriting.

## 🛠️ Technical Foundation
While this is a UI/UX case study, the design is deeply informed by its robust Flutter architecture, utilizing:
*   **Google ML Kit** for Text Recognition
*   **Mobile Scanner** for precise QR decoding
*   **Socket.io & WebSockets** for instant data transmission
*   **Provider** for seamless state management
